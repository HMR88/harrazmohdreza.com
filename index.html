I help organizations get more from the systems they already have.

I build the bridges between the systems organizations already own. Most teams already have the data they need. The problem is that it lives in systems that were never built to talk to each other, and my job is to fix that. Almost everything a business tracks has a geographic component, whether that's a location, a region, or a route. I build upon those connections to help businesses better understand the wealth of information they have access to and help them make better sense of it all. That means less time trying to figure out what all the data means, and more time understanding the business value that it can hold.
