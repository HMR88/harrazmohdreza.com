About

A technologist who treats data as something to respect — and connect.

I build the bridges between the systems organizations already own. Most teams don’t need more data — they need their existing data to finally speak the same language. Almost everything they track has a geographic component — a location, a region, a route — and that shared thread is exactly what I use to tie scattered, siloed information into one cohesive source of truth people can act on.
